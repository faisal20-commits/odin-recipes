lasagna

Description

lasagna is one of the oldest and most celebrated traditional Italian dishes,

featuring rich layers of broad pasta sheets baked to perfection.

This hearty meal is crafted by alternating layers of savory Bolognese sauce made with seasoned ground meat and tomatoes creamy Bechamel sauce,

and a generous mix of melted Mozzarella and Parmesan cheeses that give it a golden, irresistible crust.

Ingredients

  • Ground beef, chopped onion, and minced garlic
  • Tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, and Italian herbs (oregano and basil)
  • Butter, all-purpose flour, and whole milk for the Bechamel sauce
  • Lasagna pasta sheets
  • Shredded Mozzarella and grated Parmesan cheese

Steps

  1. Simmer the ground beef with onion, garlic, tomato paste, and herbs until fully cooked.
  2. In a separate pan, melt the butter, whisk in the flour, and gradually add milk to create a smooth Bechamel sauce.
  3. Spread a thin layer of sauce in a baking dish, then assemble alternating layers of pasta sheets, meat sauce, Bechamel, and Mozzarella.
  4. Bake in a preheated oven at 180°C (350°F) for 35 to 40 minutes until golden and bubbling.
