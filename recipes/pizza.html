Italian Pizza

Description

Traditional Italian Neapolitan pizza is a world-renowned culinary masterpiece, celebrated for its simplicity, quality ingredients, and distinct texture.

It features a hand-stretched dough baked to perfection in a very hot oven, resulting in a soft, airy crust with signature charred spots.

Topped with a vibrant tomato sauce, melting fresh mozzarella, and aromatic basil leaves, every slice offers an authentic taste of Italy

Ingredients

  • Artisanal pizza dough
  • Rich crushed tomato sauce (San Marzano style)
  • Fresh mozzarella cheese, cut into slices or chunks
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Fresh green basil leaves

Steps

  1. Stretch the fermented pizza dough by hand into a round disc on a floured surface, leaving the edges slightly thicker.
  2. Spread a thin layer of crushed tomato sauce evenly across the center, then distribute chunks of fresh mozzarella.
  3. Bake in a blazing hot oven or stone pizza oven until the crust is puffed and lightly charred, and the cheese is bubbling.
  4. Remove from the oven, drizzle with olive oil, garnish immediately with fresh basil leaves, and slice.
